Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How do I book a site at Cedar Ridge?

You can request a reservation through the form above, or call us at (828) 555-0142. We confirm all bookings within 24 hours, and no deposit is required to hold a request while we check availability.

Are pets allowed?

Yes — well-behaved pets are welcome at no extra charge. We have a fenced off-leash dog park, waste stations on every loop, and a short list of restricted breeds detailed in our welcome packet.

What size RVs can you accommodate?

We have pull-through sites up to 75 feet and back-in sites up to 35 feet, with 50, 30, and 20 amp service. If your rig is unusually long or wide, give us a call and we'll match you to the right spot.

How far are you from downtown Asheville?

Approximately 15 minutes (8 miles) by car. The Blue Ridge Parkway is 12 miles, Biltmore Estate is 9 miles, and the entrance to Pisgah National Forest is about 18 miles south.

Is there a cancellation policy?

Cancellations made 7+ days before arrival receive a full refund. Within 7 days, we'll refund the deposit minus a one-night fee. Weather-related cancellations are always handled case-by-case.
